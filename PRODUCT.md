# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Visitors, customers, and operators checking whether Viper services are available before retrying a request or reporting a problem. They need a fast, trustworthy answer on desktop or mobile without signing in.

## Product Purpose

Viper Status publishes the current health of the Viper portfolio and customer-facing products from one canonical domain. It summarizes Uptime Kuma monitor data, active incidents, maintenance, recent checks, and 24-hour uptime without exposing monitoring credentials.

Success means a visitor can identify the affected product and its current state within a few seconds. Operators continue managing monitors and incidents in Uptime Kuma.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, direct.

The interface should feel related to ViperCapture while using the familiar information hierarchy of strong public status products such as OpenAI Status and Discord Status.

## Anti-references

- Generic Uptime Kuma theming with no Viper identity.
- Marketing-page heroes, decorative dashboards, fake precision, and invented uptime history.
- Dense infrastructure detail, internal control-plane names, or public exposure of legacy redirects.
- Green-heavy monitoring templates that make every healthy state visually loud.
- Motion or effects that compete with incident information.

## Design Principles

1. State first. Current availability is always the strongest element.
2. Evidence over reassurance. Show real checks, uptime, and incidents without fabricated history.
3. Public products only. Hide internal infrastructure and implementation detail.
4. Brand through restraint. Use Viper typography, cobalt, spacing, and surfaces without turning status data into marketing.
5. Fail honestly. Stale or unavailable monitoring data must be labeled clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for state. Support keyboard navigation, screen readers, system light and dark preferences, reduced motion, 200% zoom, narrow mobile viewports, and high-contrast focus states.
